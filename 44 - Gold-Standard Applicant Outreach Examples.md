# 44 - Gold-Standard Applicant Outreach Examples

## Purpose

This is the approved-example library for research-powered applicant outreach. It teaches the system what strong execution sounds like: specific, useful, memorable, direct, and human.

This is not a folder for generic “templates with a name field.” Every entry must be grounded in the actual applicant context, actual provider state, and a message Jason would stand behind.

## Entry Rules

Add an example only when it is one of the following:

- Sent by Jason and considered successful or representative
- Explicitly approved by Jason as a gold-standard draft
- A synthetic training pattern clearly labeled as synthetic and not presented as a real applicant communication

For each example, preserve the context that made the copy work. A great sentence without its process state is just a bumper sticker.

## Synthetic Pattern A — Service Business Owner — Documents Missing

**Training pattern only. Not a real applicant or sent message.**

### Context

- Applicant/business: owner of a local service business with visible active jobs and a real operating website.
- State: provider needs the specific recent bank statements identified in the alert.
- Primary action: send the named statements through the provider’s requested path.

### Example

Subject: The boring document standing between you and an answer

Hi [First Name],

I took a look at [Business]. You are clearly out in the world doing real work—not trying to win a prize for keeping paperwork in the corner until it develops its own ecosystem.

The current hold-up is simple: [exact statement period/document]. Once that lands through the requested Giggle/BankBreezy path, the file can keep moving instead of sitting in administrative purgatory.

Send that one item today, then reply **done** here. If the provider email is missing or the upload step is the problem, say that plainly and we’ll take the shortest route around it.

Jason

### Why it works

- The business context proves attention without overexplaining it.
- The joke is aimed at bureaucracy, not the applicant.
- The exact document is the CTA; “reply done” closes the loop.

## Synthetic Pattern B — Coach/Team Builder — Plaid Incomplete

**Training pattern only. Not a real applicant or sent message.**

### Context

- Applicant/business: coaching or sports-related operator.
- State: Giggle email delivered; Plaid connection incomplete.
- Primary action: open the existing Giggle email and connect the primary operating account through Plaid.

### Example

Subject: One unrun play is holding up the drive

Hi [First Name],

You have already moved the ball down the field. The file is now waiting on one unglamorous play: open the Giggle email and connect the primary operating account through Plaid.

That gives the system what it needs to actually evaluate the lane. Leaving it half-finished is letting a tiny administrative penalty control the scoreboard, which is a ridiculous way to lose momentum.

Handle that step, then reply **done**. If the Giggle email got swallowed by the inbox void, reply **resend** and we’ll get you pointed back to the right place.

Jason

### Why it works

- The metaphor comes from the applicant’s actual world.
- It names the real mechanism: Giggle email → Plaid → evaluation.
- It has one action and one recovery path.

## Synthetic Pattern C — Founder/Operator — Qualification Reset

**Training pattern only. Not a real applicant or sent message.**

### Context

- Applicant/business: identifiable founder with a real operating brand, but intake did not establish the correct funding lane.
- State: request received; qualification facts missing.
- Primary action: send the three inputs that determine the route.

### Example

Subject: Let’s not make capital a guessing contest

Hi [First Name],

I spent a few minutes looking at [Business]. You are building a real operating machine, which means the funding conversation should start with the facts that control the answer—not a carnival of vague “pre-qualifications.”

Reply with three things: average monthly revenue, time in business, and what the capital is meant to do in the next 90 days. That is enough to identify the lane worth pursuing and kill the ones that would waste your time.

If the answer is “we are not there yet,” say that too. A clean read beats applying to the entire internet and hoping somebody mistakes urgency for strategy.

Jason

### Why it works

- It explains why the information is needed.
- It frames qualification as strategic filtering rather than bureaucracy.
- It gives the applicant permission to answer honestly.

## Curation Checklist

Before approving a new example, confirm:

- The research angle was actually relevant.
- The operational action was correct for the applicant’s state.
- The message had one primary CTA.
- The tone had edge without becoming performative.
- The message did not promise an approval or misstate provider mechanics.
- The outcome or Jason’s approval makes it worth teaching to the system.

## Maintenance Rule

When an approved email produces a strong response, add it here with the exact context and outcome. When an example becomes stale because provider mechanics change, retain it for voice learning but label the operational step as superseded.
